The cost of scattered work

Serious buyers do not wait for a quiet hour.

Inquiries arrive while you are in a showing. Notes live in texts, inboxes, and a CRM you barely have time to open. Listing copy and follow-up compete with the next appointment. Nothing is “live” on this page—but the problem is ordinary, and it is the reason Apex exists.

The operating path

One sequence, drafts first, send only with you.

  1. Intake A new inquiry is named, qualified in notes, and placed where you can see it.
  2. Follow-up A queue lists who needs a message today. Drafts wait for your approval.
  3. Preparation A consultation or listing packet is assembled from what you already know.
  4. Presence Caption and listing-page copy are drafted. Publishing is never assumed.
  5. Dates Inspection, financing, and closing reminders surface before they become urgent.
