**Internet Buyer Lead Scripts, Qualifying Framework & Objection Handling Playbook**
*Covering: Naples • Fort Myers • Bonita Springs • Estero • Cape Coral • Marco Island*

## 🎯 The Four Goals of Every Call
1. **Build rapport**
2. **Uncover motivation and pain points**
3. **Establish their timeline**
4. **Book the appointment**

### ✔️ What You Must Capture on Every Call (Priority Ranked):
* **Motivation / Pain Points**
* **Timeline**
* **Preferred Area or Community**
* **Use** (Primary, Vacation, Investment)
* **Property Type** (Single Family, Condo, Townhome)
* **Must-Haves** (Pool, Gulf Access, Garage, HOA/Non-HOA, View)
* **Price Range**
* **Finance or Cash**
* **Decision Makers**

## 📞 Phase 1: The Opening — First 15 Seconds
* **The Standard Opener:**
  > *"Hi [Lead Name], this is [Your Name] in [City Name]. I'm the one who's been sending you homes and I was double checking to see — are you still looking?"*
* **New Lead:**
  > *"I'm the one who will be sending you homes in [City Name], I'm double checking to see, is moving still the gameplan?"*
* **Re-engage:**
  > *"Last we spoke, you had plans of making a move and it seemed like things were on a bit of a pause… how are things looking as of now?"*
* **If they say they haven't received listings:**
  > *"On my end it shows everything's been sending out. I'll get that taken care of before the end of our call. But while I have you — tell me, when the time is right, are you thinking an investment property or a place for yourself?"*

---

## 🔍 Phase 2: Discovery & Qualifying (Listen, Acknowledge, Repeat Back)
* **Criteria Discovery:**
  * *"Are you thinking an investment property or a place for yourself?"*
  * *"Are you exclusively looking in [Area] or open to nearby areas as well?"*
  * *"How many bedrooms and bathrooms are you looking at?"*
  * *"Ballpark, what sort of price range are you looking at?"*
  * *"Will you need to sell any property in order to make this move?"*
  * *"Are you already local or where are you living as of now?"*
  * *"What lifestyle activities are important to you? Golf? Boating? Tennis?"*
* **Uncovering Motivation:**
  > *"I haven't asked you the most important question yet, of all the reasons for you to make a move, what is it that has you looking in the first place?"*
* **Go Deeper Technique:**
  > *"What would [downsizing] mean for you? Tell me more about XYZ. Why is XYZ important to you?"*
* **Lock in Timeline:**
  > *"If everything goes according to plan, when would you be moved into your next home?"*
  > *"Devil's advocate, let's say that I find you [CRITERIA] which allows you to [MOTIVATION], and it shows up in 3 months... would you want to make a move on it or 100% that's too soon?"*

---

## 🏡 Phase 3: The Sell-to-Buy Scenario (Double Appointment)
* **Normalize:**
  > *"Most of our clients are in that exact same situation — they need to sell their current home in order to purchase the new one. Our team can absolutely help you navigate that."*
* **Listing Transition:**
  > *"So it sounds like for you, the next step would be looking into getting your current home on the market. And for that, you'd need to know what your home is worth, correct?"*
* **Property Intel:** Flooring, counters, HVAC/AC age, roof age, exact property address.
* **Book Double Appointment:**
  > *"What would make the most sense is if I went ahead and scheduled an appointment for you to meet with our lead listing specialist. She'll be able to give you a really accurate idea of the market value of your current home. Would Thursday at 4 PM work for you?"*

---

## 📅 Phase 4: Booking the Appointment (The Two-Option Close)
* **Rapport Bridge:**
  > *"I've asked you a lot of questions — do you have any questions for me? Anything we haven't covered today that we should have?"*
* **Boots on the Ground (Out of Town Leads):**
  > *"I'll be your boots on the ground, so we can look virtually before you're ever out here and determine what is worth coming to take a look at."*
* **The Two-Option Close:**
  > *"The natural next step is for us to meet in person and before you know it you'll [REPEAT MOTIVATION]. Let's get together here in the office, say this Saturday around 11 a.m. or Sunday around 9 a.m., which is best?"*

## 🛡️ Objection Handling Master Toolkit

| Objection | Nikki's Playbook Script |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"I'm just browsing."** | *"Awesome — what are you browsing for?"* (Mirror their word) |
| **"Take me off your list / Not interested"** | *"Oh, is that because you already found something, or are you just putting it on the back burner for now?"* |
| **"Not right now / Not ready yet"** | *"I'll continue to send you listings, and as we get closer I'll only send what you're interested in. Tell me, are you looking for an investment or a home for yourself?"* |
| **The Golden Question (Timing)** | ⭐ *"Let me ask you this — if I found you the perfect home, would you want to know about it?"* |
| **"I can't talk right now."** | *"OK, well, while I have you — [ask immediate qualification question]"* |
| **"Saving for a down payment"** | 1. *"How much saved as of now?"* <br> 2. *"What monthly payment?"* <br> 3. *"How will you know when you have enough saved to move forward?"* |
| **"In a lease / Renting"** | *"It's possible we find you something in the exact range of what you pay in rent — except instead of paying someone else, you're paying yourself."* |
| **"Waiting for rates to drop"** | *"What sort of interest rate would you be comfortable with? Have you considered builder rate buydown incentives?"* |
| **"Friend/Cousin is an agent"** | *"This is the largest transaction in most people's lives. Just like with serious health matters, you get a second opinion. I'd love to be that second set of eyes for you."* |
| **"Already signed with an agent"** | *"Understood. If you ever find you're not getting the level of expert service you deserve, I'll send you my information. Save my name as Rosie Naples Real Estate."* |
| **Life Situations (Divorce / Loss / Job)** | *"This is one thing I can take off your plate. Tell me — what can I do to make this easier for you?"* |

## 💡 The 10 Core Principles
1. **Never Tell Them What They Signed Up For**
2. **Listen, Acknowledge, Repeat Back (Except Objections)**
3. **Mirror Their Language**
4. **Personalize Everything**
5. **Always Go One Level Deeper**
6. **The Market = Their Personal Impact**
7. **Take Things Off Their Plate**
8. **Use the Two-Option Close ("Which", not "Whether")**
9. **Enthusiasm and Presence**
10. **Plant Seeds, Don't Burn Bridges**
